How to read the atlas

These are documentary events, not findings about professional fault. A coloured track indicates relevance to a subject, not independent corroboration. “Retrospective account” refers to evidence written after the event described. Month-only or year-only dates intentionally remain imprecise. Links point to the numbered archival collection; no background data service is required.
